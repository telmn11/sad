Questions

1. What are different ways to style your form?

We can use CSS to change colors, borders, padding, margins, fonts and backgrounds. We can also use flex and media queries.

2. Why does the professor like to use flex?

Flex makes it easier to organize labels and fields in rows. It also helps keep everything aligned and organized.

3. What makes for a good mobile form?

A good mobile form is easy to read and use. Fields should be big enough to tap and the layout should fit a small screen.
