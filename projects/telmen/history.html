Where Did Chess Begin?

Chess has a long and interesting history. Most historians connect its origins to an ancient Indian game called Chaturanga, which appeared around the 6th century.

The Development of Chess

India

An early form of chess called Chaturanga developed in India around the 6th century.

Persia

The game spread to Persia and became known as Shatranj.

Europe

Chess reached Europe and its rules gradually changed into the modern game.

Modern Chess

International competitions and official rules helped chess become a worldwide game.

Why Chess Became Popular

Chess became popular because it challenges people to think ahead, solve problems, and make careful decisions. Today, people play chess in schools, clubs, tournaments, and online platforms.
