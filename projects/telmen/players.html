Great Chess Players

Magnus Carlsen

A Norwegian grandmaster who became World Chess Champion in 2013. He is known for his strong endgame skills.

Garry Kasparov

A famous chess player who became World Champion in 1985 and held the title until 2000.

Bobby Fischer

An American chess player who became World Champion in 1972.

Judit Polgar

A Hungarian grandmaster widely recognized as one of the strongest women chess players in history.

Viswanathan Anand

An Indian grandmaster and five-time World Chess Champion.

Hou Yifan

A Chinese grandmaster and four-time Women's World Chess Champion.

What Makes a Great Chess Player?

Great chess players develop strong calculation skills, patience, creativity, and the ability to learn from mistakes. They study different positions and practice regularly.
