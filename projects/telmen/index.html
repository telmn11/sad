About Chess

Chess is one of the most popular strategy games in the world. Two players compete on a board with 64 squares and 16 pieces each. The main goal is to checkmate the opponent's king.

Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because chess has been one of my favorite games since I was a child. I grew up playing chess with my grandfather, and it became a special part of my childhood. Through chess, I learned to think carefully, be patient, and plan ahead.
