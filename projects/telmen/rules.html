The Six Chess Pieces

Each player starts with 16 pieces. Every piece has its own movement and purpose.

♔

King

Moves one square in any direction.

♕

Queen

Moves horizontally, vertically, and diagonally.

♖

Rook

Moves horizontally and vertically.

♗

Bishop

Moves diagonally across the board.

♘

Knight

Moves in an L-shape and can jump over pieces.

♙

Pawn

Usually moves forward one square and captures diagonally.

Important Chess Terms

Check

The king is under attack.

Checkmate

The king is under attack and cannot escape.

Stalemate

A player has no legal moves, but their king is not in check.

Castling

A special move involving the king and rook.

Promotion

A pawn can become another piece when it reaches the last rank.
